Gen 1 · All-rounder · In simulation now. First to be built (goal 3).

A rear-drive mower the size of a YardForce Classic 500, which it's modelled on. The real Ion is a used YardForce with its control board swapped for an OpenMower kit, our Raspberry Pi brain, centimetre GPS and a 3D lidar. The body, blade guard, battery and dock are the factory's, already certified, which is why it goes first.

Ion is in simulation and next to be built. Parts and prices are estimates, in US dollars (Nov 2026). Drawings are generated from the profile the simulator uses.

Build plan

  1. Sensors on a cartPi, GNSS and IMU on a push-cart; map the lawn's edge by walking it.Test: Static RTK fix within 2 cm for 10 minutes
  2. Board swapReplace the YardForce board with the OpenMower kit; blade motor unplugged.Test: Wheels turn on command; stop button cuts them
  3. Brain inMount the Pi, GNSS mast, IMU and lidar; run the same ROS 2 stack as the sim.Test: Drives a 2 m square and returns within 5 cm
  4. Map and dockRecord the lawn and keep-outs; dock and charge.Test: Ten dockings in a row
  5. Mow, blade offFull missions with the blade disconnected.Test: Coverage report ≥ 95%
  6. Blade onOnly after every check in hardware/safety.md passes; supervised first.Test: Interlock tests pass
