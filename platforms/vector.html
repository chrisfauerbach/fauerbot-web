Gen 2 · Brawler · Concept. Simulated.

An 82 × 74 cm skid-steer robot on four hoverboard hub motors, with a 46 cm blade between the axles. Four driven wheels climb roots and molehills a caster would hang up on. Skid-steer turns by scrubbing its tyres sideways, so it uses more power in turns and its wheel odometry is worse: GNSS and the IMU matter more.

Vector is a concept: simulated, not built. Parts and prices are estimates, in US dollars (Nov 2026). Drawings are generated from the profile the simulator uses.
