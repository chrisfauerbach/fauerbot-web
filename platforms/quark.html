Gen 2 · Scout · Concept. Simulated.

A 46 × 42 cm robot with a 24 cm blade for small city lawns and the narrow side passages bigger robots can't get through. It fits through a 55 cm gap. Its own small body is 3D-printed or moulded, with two geared wheel motors, a caster, and the same brain and sensors as every Fauerbot.

Quark is a concept: simulated, not built. Parts and prices are estimates, in US dollars (Nov 2026). Drawings are generated from the profile the simulator uses.
