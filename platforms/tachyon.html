Gen 3 · Speedster · Concept. Simulated.

A 1 m wide robot with two 36 cm blades side by side: a 70 cm cut, nearly twice Ion's, at up to 0.8 m/s. Two big drive wheels and two casters. It's for large open lawns, where the time to cover the area matters more than reaching every corner. The width is the price: it needs a 1.1 m gap, and the uncut band along fences is wider.

Tachyon is a concept: simulated, not built. Parts and prices are estimates, in US dollars (Nov 2026). Drawings are generated from the profile the simulator uses.
