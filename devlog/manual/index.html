    Build manual

    The goal: someone who isn't us can buy the parts, build a robot, and prove it is safe and working, with the same pass/fail style as the simulator reports in the devlog.

    This is a hobby project, not a certified product

    You are responsible for whatever you build. Until the blade-on checklist passes, the blade stays off the motor. A robot that drives the lawn without cutting is still a complete localization and navigation project.

    How this manual is organised

    It follows the four kinds of documentation (tutorials, how-to guides, reference, explanation), because a first build, a blade swap and a torque value are different jobs and need different pages.

    Kind You want to… Pages
    Tutorial build your first robot, start to finish H1: bench and push-cart · H2: brain transplant · H3: blade on (H2 and H3 are drafts until the hardware arrives)
    How-to do one job Buy the parts · Check parts on arrival
    Reference look something up End-of-line test · Verification and acceptance tests · Build record and quality
    Explanation know why the devlog: every design decision has a post

    The rules every page follows

    1. One action per step, with the tools it needs and roughly how long it takes.
    2. Safety notes are red boxes, inline, before the step they apply to: never in an appendix.
    3. Every sub-assembly ends at a checkpoint: a short test with a pass/fail result. Fail = stop. Don't build on top of a failed checkpoint; use the troubleshooting notes on that page.
    4. Every checkpoint is recorded in the robot's build record, so a robot's history can be traced from its serial number.
    5. Verification uses the simulator's reports, run on the real robot with the same thresholds (or documented, looser ones). If the sim says "localization within 5 cm RMS", the real robot has to show it too.

    The build stages

    Stage What you have at the end Blade
    H1 Bench and push-cart Pi 5 + RTK GNSS + IMU on a garden cart; a surveyed lawn boundary none
    H2 Brain transplant A used robot mower chassis driven by our software, docking and charging disconnected and taped
    H3 Blade on Supervised mowing after the safety gate passes on, after gate 3
    H4 Custom chassis Our own frame, motors and battery (optional, later) on
